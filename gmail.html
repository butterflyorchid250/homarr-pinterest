Homarr Dashboard · Personal Gmail Integration

Homarr Dashboard is a self-hosted, owner-operated home dashboard. Its private Gmail integration lets the owner access and manage their own Gmail account within the dashboard. This public information page is not the dashboard or a Gmail sign-in page.

The integration uses Google's OAuth 2.0 consent flow and official Gmail API. Only the account owner authorises access.

What the integration does

  • Display a small list of recent inbox messages, subjects, senders and unread count.
  • Open and read email messages, including supported HTML and images.
  • Compose, send and reply to email when the owner requests it.
  • Mark messages read or unread, star or unstar, archive, and move messages to Trash when the owner chooses those actions.

Privacy and access

Gmail message contents and OAuth credentials are not published on this website. Access to the actual dashboard and its email features is restricted to the owner's private setup. Gmail tokens are held server-side, not in this public GitHub Pages content.

The integration is for personal, non-commercial use only. It does not provide email services to other people, sell Gmail information or display advertising.
