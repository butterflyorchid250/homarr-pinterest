Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Pinterest data

This application is a private, self-hosted personal dashboard integration used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Pinterest boards and Pins through Pinterest OAuth and Pinterest's official API solely to display that content inside the owner's private dashboard.

Information stored

Pinterest OAuth credentials and tokens required for the integration are stored only on the owner's private server. They are not intentionally exposed to the public website or browser clients.

Sharing

Pinterest data, OAuth credentials, and tokens are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Advertising and tracking

The application does not use advertising, behavioural tracking, or third-party analytics.

Changes to Pinterest content

The integration is read-only and does not create, modify, schedule, or delete Pinterest Pins or boards.

Access and revocation

Pinterest access may be revoked through the owner's Pinterest account settings at any time.

Scope

This application is intended only for single-user personal use and is not offered as a service to other users.
